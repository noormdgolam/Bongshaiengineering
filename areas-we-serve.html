Frequently Asked Questions

What Do Clients Frequently Ask About Our Nationwide Service Coverage?

Does Bongshai Engineering & Construction work outside Dhaka?

Yes. While our head office is in Uttara, Dhaka, Bongshai E&C deploys full project teams, heavy equipment, and remote site offices to any division of Bangladesh, including Chattogram, Rajshahi, Khulna, Sylhet, Rangpur, Barishal, and Mymensingh.

Which districts of Bangladesh does Bongshai E&C serve?

Bongshai E&C serves all 64 districts across all 8 divisions of Bangladesh. See the full district list by division on this page, or contact us directly to discuss your specific district.

How quickly can Bongshai E&C mobilize a project team to a new district?

Our engineering team reviews project scope within 24 hours of inquiry and provides a preliminary budget-level estimate within 48-72 hours. For projects requiring a site visit, our team can typically be on-site within 5 business days, regardless of district.

In which districts has Bongshai E&C already completed projects?

Bongshai E&C has completed major projects in Dhaka (including a 20-storied hotel tower, military stadium, and Purbachal Expressway piling works), Chattogram (a 16-storied commercial and medical center), Rangpur (cantonment and Saidpur defense facilities), and Rajshahi (the 100 MW Katakhali power plant). Our full portfolio is available on the Executed Projects page.

Does Bongshai E&C provide remote site offices and crew accommodation nationwide?

Yes. Bongshai E&C is equipped to establish remote site offices, crew accommodation, and logistics support for any project location across Bangladesh, supported by our company-owned heavy equipment fleet and centralized Dhaka HQ project management and QA oversight.

How do I request a quote for a project outside Dhaka?

Email your project scope, district, and approximate floor area or scope of work to info@bongshai.com, or use the RFQ estimator and contact form on our Contact Us page. Our team will confirm mobilization details for your specific district as part of the estimate.
