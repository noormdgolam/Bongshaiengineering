Our Mission & Vision

Bongshai Engineering & Construction’s mission is to execute complex engineering projects with international safety and quality standards. Our vision is built on honesty, integrity, and prioritizing absolute customer satisfaction through time-bound, cost-effective delivery across Bangladesh.

Last updated: August 8, 2026 • Reviewed & Approved by SMA Awal, Chief Engineer, Bongshai Engineering & Construction

👁️

What is the corporate vision of Bongshai E&C?

The primary objective of BONGSHAI E&C is to concentrate on providing high-quality, customer-oriented job satisfaction and time-bound services, which is the company’s top priority. Honesty and integrity are our committed assurance to valued customers to achieve goals in the larger interest.

To provide the best services with quality and workmanship, with a time-framed delivery programme at most comprehensive pricing with marginal profit, is our firm commitment. "Name and Fame with customer satisfaction is our motto and achievement."

🎯

What is the core mission of our engineering firm?

Bongshai Engineering & Construction Co. Ltd. is established with the vision to donate the latest international design and building engineering codes and world-class construction presentation at home and abroad, preferring to lead any others in the industry. We have the experience and resources to execute complex projects on time with international standards of quality, safety, and reliability.

Our mission lies in customer service and customer satisfaction. We offer our best possible attention and care to our valued customers. Customer satisfaction is our wealth and mission. Our aim is to provide service brilliance to our customers, society, and nation while establishing a reputation of excellence through people, products, and places.

We ensure customer satisfaction by maintaining a time-framed delivery programme, communicating honestly with clients, and executing workmanship to the highest industry standards with marginal profit.
