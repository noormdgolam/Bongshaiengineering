Who Is the Right Construction Company in Dhaka for Your Project?

Bongshai Engineering & Construction (BEC) is headquartered in Uttara, Dhaka, and has completed major civil, structural, and piling projects across the city — from Mirpur to Purbachal to the Banani/Gulshan commercial corridor. As our home district, Dhaka is where our full engineering staff, QA/QC teams, and heavy equipment fleet are based, giving Dhaka-based clients the fastest mobilization and site-visit turnaround of anywhere in Bangladesh.

Frequently Asked Questions

What Do Dhaka Clients Frequently Ask?

Does Bongshai E&C operate in Dhaka?

Yes. Dhaka is Bongshai E&C's home district — our head office is in Uttara, Sector 10, and our full engineering, QA/QC, and project management teams are based here.

What Dhaka-area projects has Bongshai E&C completed?

Completed Dhaka-area projects include a 20-storied hotel tower in Banani/Gulshan, a military indoor stadium, Mirpur housing piling works, and Purbachal Expressway canal retention piling for RAJUK.

Which parts of Dhaka does Bongshai E&C serve?

Bongshai E&C serves all of Dhaka District, including Uttara, Mirpur, Banani, Gulshan, and Purbachal, as well as the neighboring Dhaka Division districts of Gazipur and Narayanganj.

How fast can Bongshai E&C schedule a Dhaka site visit?

Since our HQ is in Dhaka, site visits for Dhaka-area projects can typically be scheduled faster than the standard 5-business-day window quoted for other districts.
