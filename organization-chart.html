Company Profile • Leadership Structure

Organization Chart

Bongshai Engineering & Construction operates under a structured organizational hierarchy led by CEO S. M. Awal. Our organization divides into core executive sections for engineering and procurement, and specialized field execution teams ensuring strict QA/QC and HSE compliance on site.

Last updated: August 8, 2026 • Reviewed & Approved by SMA Awal, Chief Engineer, Bongshai Engineering & Construction

C E O (S. M. Awal)

What core executive sections manage our operations?

Engineering Section

Civil, Structural, Architectural, Electrical & Mechanical Design Engineers.

Project Manager

Site administration, scheduling, resource management, and client coordination.

Public Relation (PR)

Institutional liaison, government relations, and media communications.

Procurement Section

Global material supply chain, steel sourcing, and equipment leasing.

Research & Development (R&D)

Advanced structural engineering software, BIM integration, and sustainable codes.

What field execution and quality teams operate on our sites?

Construction Team

Site foremen, steel erectors, masons, carpenters, and piling crews.

QA/QC Team

Quality Assurance & Quality Control inspectors enforcing AISC and BNBC standards.

HSE Team (Health, Safety & Environment)

Safety officers monitoring site zero-accident compliance and environmental safety.

Material & Admin Section

Inventory control, warehousing, heavy machinery maintenance, and logistics.

HR Section (Human Resources)

Workforce welfare, skill training, safety certification, and staff benefits.

What do clients frequently ask about our organizational structure?

Who leads the Bongshai Engineering executive team?

The entire organizational hierarchy is led by CEO S. M. Awal, who provides strategic direction to both the core executive sections and the field execution teams.

How is quality and safety enforced across the organization?

We maintain independent QA/QC and HSE teams that report directly to executive management, ensuring that safety compliance and build quality are never compromised by construction schedule pressures.
