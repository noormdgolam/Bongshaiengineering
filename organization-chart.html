What do clients frequently ask about our organizational structure?

Who leads the Bongshai Engineering executive team?

The entire organizational hierarchy is led by CEO S. M. Awal, who provides strategic direction to both the core executive sections and the field execution teams.

How is quality and safety enforced across the organization?

We maintain independent QA/QC and HSE teams that report directly to executive management, ensuring that safety compliance and build quality are never compromised by construction schedule pressures.
