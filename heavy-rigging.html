Heavy Rigging & Lifting Division

Bongshai Engineering & Construction provides certified heavy rigging and critical lifting services across Bangladesh. We utilize our company-owned fleet of Japanese crawler cranes, including 50-Ton Kobelco and 25-Ton Hitachi units, for precision industrial machinery positioning and high-altitude steel truss erection.

Last updated: August 8, 2026 • Reviewed & Approved by SMA Awal, Chief Engineer, Bongshai Engineering & Construction

What certified rigging and critical lift operations do we perform in Bangladesh?

Bongshai Engineering & Construction maintains an elite heavy lifting division staffed by certified master riggers and experienced crane operators. We specialize in critical lift engineering for power plants, heavy steel industrial sheds, sub-station transformer positioning, and high-altitude truss placement across Bangladesh.

What crawler cranes are in our heavy lifting fleet?

50-Ton Kobelco Hydraulic Crawler Crane

Japanese heavy lattice-boom crawler crane for long-reach structural steel truss erection, heavy boiler hoisting, and deep foundation piling support.

25-Ton Hitachi Heavy Crawler Crane

Versatile Japanese crawler crane designed for tight industrial jobsite mobility, medium-heavy steel beam placement, and precast concrete panel positioning.

Japanese H35 (5-Ton) Diesel Hammer Sets

Heavy-impact diesel pile driving hammer rigs for driving 300mm to 500mm pre-cast concrete piles in challenging soil profiles.

Heavy Winches, Hoists & Chain Blocks

Industrial winches, 0.5 m³ grab buckets, 3-Ton to 5-Ton chain pulley blocks (6 sets), and heavy wire rope slings for micro-alignment of machinery.

What do clients frequently ask about our heavy rigging services?

What equipment is used for micro-alignment of heavy machinery?

We utilize industrial winches, 3-Ton to 5-Ton chain pulley blocks (6 sets), and heavy wire rope slings for the micro-alignment and precision positioning of heavy machinery.

What piling hammer rigs do you deploy?

We deploy two sets of Japanese H35 Diesel Hammer rigs featuring a 5-Ton Impact Energy Ram for driving pre-cast concrete piles.

Do you rent heavy lifting equipment to third parties?

Our heavy equipment fleet is primarily dedicated to Bongshai E&C's in-house mega projects to ensure complete schedule reliability without depending on third-party availability.
