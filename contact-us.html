How do you get a free project estimate from Bongshai E&C?

BONGSHAI E&C provides free preliminary project estimates for all construction types — civil, industrial, PEB steel, RCC, electrical, heavy rigging, and architectural works. The estimation process is straightforward and designed to give you accurate budget guidance as quickly as possible.

1
Share Project Scope — Send a brief description of your project to info@bongshai.com or call 01714104940. Include building type, approximate floor area, number of stories, and intended use.
2
Technical Review — Our engineering team reviews your scope within 24 hours and prepares a list of clarifying questions about structural standards, material specifications, site access, and construction program expectations.
3
Preliminary Estimate — We provide a budget-level estimate within 48–72 hours of receiving complete scope information. For complex projects, a site visit may be scheduled within 5 business days to assess ground conditions, access constraints, and existing structure interface points.
4
Detailed Proposal — Following scope finalization and site visit, a detailed engineering proposal with unit rates, construction schedule, payment milestones, and warranty terms is submitted within 7–10 business days.

Preliminary budget estimates are typically provided within 48–72 hours of receiving complete project scope information. For complex projects requiring a site visit, the estimate is prepared within 7–10 business days from the site visit date. All estimates are provided at no charge.

Does BONGSHAI E&C work on projects outside Dhaka?

Yes. BONGSHAI E&C deploys full project teams to any division in Bangladesh — including Chattogram, Khulna, Rajshahi, Sylhet, Mymensingh, Bogura, and beyond. The company is experienced in establishing remote site offices and managing multi-location projects simultaneously.

What information should I include when contacting BONGSHAI E&C for a project?

For the fastest response, include: building type (garment factory, industrial shed, commercial building, etc.), approximate floor area (m²), number of floors, project location, desired construction standard (BNBC 2020, Accord compliant, etc.), and intended project start date. Sending any existing drawings or sketch layouts helps our estimating team provide a more accurate initial budget.
