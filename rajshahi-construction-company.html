What Do Rajshahi Clients Frequently Ask?

Does Bongshai E&C have power-plant experience in Rajshahi?

Yes. Bongshai E&C completed the civil works for a 100 MW heavy thermal power plant in Katakhali, Rajshahi, for Energypac Power Generation Ltd.

What civil scope did Bongshai E&C deliver on the Katakhali power plant?

Heavy engine foundation monoliths, fuel tank RCC retaining bund walls, 33kV substation yard civil structures, and heavy drainage networks.

Yes — our power-plant civil engineering experience in Katakhali extends to other heavy-industrial and substation civil works across Rajshahi Division.
