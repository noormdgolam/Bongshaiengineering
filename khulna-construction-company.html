Areas We Serve • Khulna District

Can Bongshai Engineering & Construction Take On a Project in Khulna?

While Bongshai Engineering & Construction's (BEC) completed-project portfolio to date is concentrated in Dhaka, Chattogram, Rangpur, and Rajshahi, BEC is fully equipped to mobilize project teams, heavy equipment, and remote site offices to Khulna Division. Khulna's Mongla Port, shipbuilding industry, and jute/textile processing sector fall squarely within BEC's civil, structural, and industrial construction capabilities.

Last updated: August 9, 2026 • Reviewed & Approved by SMA Awal, Chief Engineer, Bongshai Engineering & Construction

Deployment Capability

What Can Bongshai E&C Deliver in Khulna?

Bongshai E&C has not yet completed a project in Khulna, and we say so plainly here — our verified project history is in Dhaka, Chattogram, Rangpur, and Rajshahi (see our full coverage map). What we can offer Khulna clients today is the same company-owned equipment fleet, 350+ certified engineering staff, and remote-site-office capability that powered those completed projects — ready to mobilize to Khulna Division for civil infrastructure, RCC building, PEB steel, and industrial construction work.

Frequently Asked Questions

What Do Khulna Clients Frequently Ask?

Has Bongshai E&C completed a project in Khulna?

Not yet — our completed-project portfolio to date is concentrated in Dhaka, Chattogram, Rangpur, and Rajshahi. We are, however, fully equipped and ready to mobilize a project team to Khulna.

What can Bongshai E&C build in Khulna?

Our core capabilities — civil infrastructure, RCC building construction, PEB steel structures, and industrial construction — apply directly to Khulna's port, shipbuilding, and jute/textile processing sectors.

Can Bongshai E&C set up a remote site office in Khulna?

Yes. We can establish a remote site office, crew accommodation, and logistics support in Khulna, the same as any other division.

Which districts of Khulna Division does Bongshai E&C serve?

All 10: Bagerhat, Chuadanga, Jashore, Jhenaidah, Khulna, Kushtia, Magura, Meherpur, Narail, and Satkhira.
