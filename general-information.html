Is Bongshai E&C fully licensed and registered for construction in Bangladesh?

BONGSHAI Engineering & Construction was established on November 15, 2008 and has since maintained full legal compliance with all applicable Bangladesh regulatory requirements for engineering and construction companies. The company operates under valid business registration, construction license, and company incorporation — maintaining active membership with four major industry associations that govern the construction sector in Bangladesh.

The company's construction license, issued by the Construction Association of Bangladesh, authorizes BONGSHAI E&C to undertake construction contracts across all civil, structural, mechanical, electrical, and industrial categories without restriction on project scale. This is a legally significant credential — only a fraction of Bangladesh's registered contractors hold an unrestricted all-category license.

Membership in the International Contractors Association of Bangladesh qualifies BONGSHAI E&C to bid on internationally funded projects (World Bank, ADB, IFC) and participate in government-to-government construction programs. The Dhaka Chamber of Commerce membership underpins the company's active engagement in trade forums, industry standards development, and business networking.

What do clients frequently ask about Bongshai E&C's general information?

When was BONGSHAI Engineering & Construction founded?

BONGSHAI Engineering & Construction was established on November 15, 2008 by CEO & Chief Engineer S. M. Awal. The company obtained its business registration in October 2009, its construction license in December 2010, and completed full company registration in September 2012.

What industry associations is BONGSHAI E&C a member of?

BONGSHAI E&C holds active membership in four organizations: the Construction Association of Bangladesh, the International Contractors Association of Bangladesh, the Chamber of Commerce of Dhaka, and the Construction Financial Co-operative. These memberships give BONGSHAI E&C access to international tender notifications, industry standards, and regulatory engagement platforms.

How many engineering disciplines does BONGSHAI E&C operate?

BONGSHAI E&C operates 8 integrated engineering disciplines: Civil Engineering, Building & Architectural, Structural Steel, Electrical, Electro-Mechanical, Mechanical, Power & Energy, and Industrial Engineering. All 8 disciplines are staffed in-house under the unified EPC&C delivery model.
