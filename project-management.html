Project Management & Controls Framework

Bongshai Engineering & Construction enforces strict turnkey project governance on all mega-projects. We ensure on-schedule and within-budget execution through Primavera P6 Critical Path Method (CPM) scheduling, precise total station GPS surveying, and Earned Value Management (EVM).

Last updated: August 8, 2026 • Reviewed & Approved by SMA Awal, Chief Engineer, Bongshai Engineering & Construction

How do we govern turnkey EPC project execution?

Bongshai Engineering & Construction deploys a multi-disciplinary Project Management Office (PMO) to oversee every phase of EPC contracting from feasibility modeling and procurement to commissioning. Utilizing Primavera P6 Professional, MS Project, and specialized structural BIM tools, our project managers enforce strict milestone compliance.

What are our core project management workstreams?

Baseline Scheduling & CPM Control

Primavera P6 WBS breakdown, Critical Path Method (CPM) tracking, S-curve progress monitoring, and float analysis to prevent timeline slippage.

Resource & Fleet Logistics Allocation

Strategic scheduling of company-owned heavy equipment (50T/25T crawler cranes, batching plants, transit mixers) and multi-skilled engineering teams across active sites.

Total Station GPS Survey Control

Millimeter-precise spatial positioning using Leica Total Stations, GPS benchmark coordination, digital leveling, and 3D structural alignment checks.

Earned Value Management (EVM)

Cost Performance Index (CPI) and Schedule Performance Index (SPI) quantitative tracking to ensure accurate cash flow forecasting and budget variance control.

How do we track project costs and budget variance?

Our PMO utilizes Earned Value Management (EVM) to quantitatively track the Cost Performance Index (CPI) and Schedule Performance Index (SPI). This methodology ensures accurate cash flow forecasting, early detection of budget variance, and transparent financial reporting to stakeholders throughout the project lifecycle.

What do clients frequently ask about our project management?

How frequently are project progress reports submitted to clients?

We provide weekly progress lookahead schedules and formal monthly executive dashboards featuring S-Curves, milestone variance analysis, site photos, and resource utilization reports.

How are project risks and supply chain delays mitigated?

Our PMO maintains an active Risk Management Register. Long-lead items (such as specialized steel plates or high-voltage switchgear) are ordered early with dual-source procurement strategies to absorb supply chain shocks.
