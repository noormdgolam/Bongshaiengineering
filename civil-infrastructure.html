Civil Infrastructure Division

What civil engineering capabilities does Bongshai E&C provide in Bangladesh?

Bongshai Engineering & Construction provides turnkey civil infrastructure development services for government infrastructure projects, industrial zones, and multi-national commercial developments. Operating with state-of-the-art hydraulic rotary drilling rigs, Japanese diesel pile hammers, batching plants, and heavy excavators, our civil engineering team ensures structural stability across complex deltaic soil conditions in Bangladesh.

What are our core technical specializations in civil infrastructure?

Landscaping & Dredging Earthwork

Large-scale site reclamation, hydraulic riverbed sand filling, dynamic compaction, and subgrade soil stabilization tailored for industrial parks and high-density structural footprints.

Cast-in-Situ Bored Piling

Deep rotary drilled bored piles up to 1200mm in diameter reaching depths over 50 meters. Complete bentonite slurry stabilization, tremie pipe concrete pouring, and sonic logging access pipes.

Pre-Cast Reinforced Piling

High-strength pre-cast concrete piles (300mm x 300mm to 500mm x 500mm) manufactured under controlled factory conditions and installed using Japanese H35 5-Ton Diesel Hammers.

Heavy Industrial Pavements

Engineered rigid RCC pavement design for heavy container yards, factory logistics aprons, asphalt binder courses, and sub-base aggregate compaction meeting RHD specifications.

Water Supply & Municipal Drainage

Industrial water distribution grids, deep storm water retention channels, RCC box culverts, effluent discharge lines, and municipal sanitary sewage collection systems.

Road, Bridge & Culvert Execution

Pre-stressed concrete girder bridges, pier caps, abutment walls, flyovers, and heavy traffic access roads engineered for heavy axel load transport.

Deep Tube Well Installation

High-yield industrial water extraction wells drilled to 300+ meters, reverse circulation drilling, stainless steel screen installation, and submersible pump setup for textile & industrial plants.

What do clients frequently ask about our civil infrastructure services?

What pile testing methods does Bongshai Engineering conduct on site?

We strictly adhere to ASTM D1143 for Static Compression Load Testing, ASTM D4945 for High Strain Dynamic Pile Testing (PDA), and Pile Integrity Testing (PIT) using sonic echo analysis to verify concrete integrity and load bearing capacity before structural handover.

How do you manage high groundwater levels during deep excavation and piling?

In marshy or high water-table soils common in Bangladesh, we utilize heavy-duty bentonite mud slurry suspension to prevent borehole collapse during rotary drilling, along with deep well dewatering systems and steel temporary casing pipes.
