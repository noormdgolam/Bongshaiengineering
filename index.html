Company Philosophy

Why do clients trust Bongshai Engineering for turnkey EPC&C execution?

BONGSHAI E&C is a Bangladesh-based Engineering & Construction Company. The Company undertakes projects on the basis of Engineering-Procurement-Construction & Commissioning (EPC&C).

We possess the experience and equipment resources to execute complex mega projects on time with international standards of quality, safety, and structural reliability. From architectural design and material supply to construction, erection, and test operation, BONGSHAI E&C handles the entire project lifecycle seamlessly.

Our Mission

To deliver world-class construction presentation with latest codes, Preferences to quality, and complete client satisfaction.

Our Vision

Concentrating on high quality, customer-oriented time-bound services with utmost honesty, integrity, and marginal profit.
