What are Bongshai Engineering's most notable executed mega projects?

What international construction experience does Bongshai E&C have?

Internationally, we provided sub-contract civil foundations, heavy equipment erection, and pipe rack assembly for the QAFCO Fertilizer Plant 5/6 expansion in Mesaieed Industrial City, Qatar, working with Mitsubishi Heavy Industries.

What types of commercial high-rise projects have we completed?

We completed the Premier Hotel Management Ltd 20-storied high-rise tower in Dhaka, executing deep bored piling, 3-level basement rafts, and post-tensioned floor slabs.

What is our capability in industrial PEB factory construction?

We deliver turnkey pre-engineered steel buildings, demonstrated by the Akij Industrial PEB Factory featuring 120,000 sq ft of area, 75-meter clear-span framing, and 25-Ton crane gantry girders.

Do we have experience executing defense and cantonment civil works?

Yes, we have executed major military infrastructure including the Jalalabad Army Cantonment Officers Mess, multi-storied accommodations, auditoriums, and parade ground civil works in Sylhet.
