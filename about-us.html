What is the engineering foundation and capacity of Bongshai E&C?

BONGSHAI Engineering & Construction (BEC) is a Bangladesh-based full-spectrum Engineering & Construction company, established on November 15, 2008, by CEO & Chief Engineer S. M. Awal. The company undertakes projects on a complete Engineering-Procurement-Construction & Commissioning (EPC&C) basis — assuming total responsibility for every phase from initial design through final handover.

Headquartered at Alordhara, Level 05, House #18, Road #18, Sector #10, Uttara C/A, Dhaka 1230, Bangladesh, BONGSHAI E&C maintains a highly active operational posture across multiple simultaneous project sites throughout the country. The company is capable of deploying over 350 certified personnel — including civil engineers, structural engineers, QC inspectors, HSE officers, skilled steel fabricators, riggers, scaffolders, and electrical technicians — to any location in Bangladesh.

Since its founding, BONGSHAI E&C has grown from a modest civil contracting firm into one of Bangladesh's recognized multi-discipline EPC&C contractors. The company's project portfolio spans industrial factories, pre-engineered steel buildings, RCC multi-story structures, electrical distribution systems, heavy mechanical rigging, and architectural finishing — serving clients from garments, textile, food processing, power generation, cement, and real estate sectors.

Highly Qualified Professional Workforce

BONGSHAI E&C employs a multi-tiered professional workforce comprising graduate engineers (B.Sc. in Civil, Structural, Electrical, and Mechanical Engineering), diploma-level supervisors and surveyors, trade-certified foremen, and BNBC-trained safety officers. All personnel receive regular training in BNBC 2020 compliance, fire safety protocol, confined space entry procedures, and equipment safety. The company's HR policy ensures every worker receives fair compensation, disciplinary clarity, and continuous skill development.

BONGSHAI E&C has extensive experience in preparation, coordination, implementation, new-build, and maintenance works. With a constant focus on client value, innovative service solutions, and international best practices, the company ensures world-class delivery on every project — regardless of scale, complexity, or location.

How does Bongshai E&C manage projects using the EPC&C methodology?

BONGSHAI E&C's EPC&C model integrates all four phases of project delivery under a single contractual responsibility, eliminating coordination gaps between designer, supplier, contractor, and commissioner. This approach reduces project risk, accelerates schedules, and controls total cost of ownership for clients.

📐

Engineering

Structural analysis, BNBC 2020 code compliance, AutoCAD 3D detailing, BIM coordination, soil investigation review, and design-for-constructability checks before any material is ordered.

🏭

Procurement

Vendor qualification, bulk material sourcing, steel section procurement (ASTM A36/A572, SS400, Grade-60 rebar), equipment hiring, and supply chain tracking across all disciplines.

On-site execution with 350+ personnel, crawler cranes, concrete pumps, and batching plants. Daily progress reporting, QC hold points, and strict HSE enforcement throughout the construction cycle.

How has Bongshai E&C grown since its founding in 2008?

BONGSHAI E&C was formally established on November 15, 2008, in Uttara, Dhaka, with a vision to bring EPC&C discipline to Bangladesh's construction sector — an approach more common in international oil & gas and industrial construction markets than in the local civil contracting landscape at the time.

Business registration was completed in October 2009, followed by the award of the full construction license in December 2010. By September 2012, the company completed its corporate registration and expanded its service divisions to include pre-engineered steel buildings (PEB), heavy rigging, and electrical engineering — creating a truly multi-discipline platform.

Through the 2010s, BONGSHAI E&C built its reputation executing complex garment factory buildings, industrial sheds, and infrastructure works for major Bangladeshi industrial groups. By the 2020s, the company had assembled one of Bangladesh's most complete private heavy construction equipment fleets — including multiple Japanese crawler cranes, concrete pumping systems, and purpose-built steel fabrication workshops — enabling it to bid competitively on projects that few local contractors could independently execute.
