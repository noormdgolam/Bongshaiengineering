Why should you build your career at Bongshai E&C?

BONGSHAI Engineering & Construction (BEC) offers engineers and skilled professionals a rare combination: technically challenging mega-project work, a structured learning environment, international engineering standards, and a direct line to senior leadership including CEO & Chief Engineer S. M. Awal. Since 2008, the company has invested consistently in its people — providing formal safety training, code compliance education, and hands-on mentoring from senior engineers with decades of EPC&C experience.

Whether you are a fresh graduate civil engineer looking for your first structural site experience, a diploma supervisor ready to lead a concrete gang on a multi-story factory project, or a certified rigger seeking to work with Japan-built crawler cranes on complex lift operations — BONGSHAI E&C has an active project pipeline that demands your skills year-round.

Safety-First Culture

Zero-tolerance HSE policy. BNBC and NFPA-based safety standards. Daily toolbox talks, PPE provision, and incident-free culture.

Continuous Learning

Regular training in BNBC 2020, AutoCAD, rigging safety, confined space, and QMS inspection plan management.

Mega-Project Exposure

Work on 13+ mega projects across civil, industrial, PEB steel, heavy rigging, and electrical disciplines in Bangladesh.

Modern Equipment

Operate Japanese crawler cranes (Kobelco, Kato), Schwing concrete pumps, and modern steel fabrication equipment.

Career Growth

Clear career ladder from Site Engineer → Project Engineer → Project Manager → Division Head with merit-based promotion.

Fair Compensation

Competitive market salaries, performance bonuses, overtime pay, site allowances, and festival bonuses.

What learning and development training does Bongshai E&C provide?

At BONGSHAI E&C, we invest in continuous skill training for graduate engineers, diploma surveyors, and certified trade operatives. All employees receive regular safety, code compliance, and structural software education. Our in-house training program covers:

✔ BNBC 2020 structural code training for site engineers and supervisors
✔ AutoCAD structural drawing and dimensioning for junior engineers
✔ Rigging safety, load chart reading, and sling selection training for riggers
✔ QMS inspection and test plan (ITP) management for QC inspectors
✔ AWS D1.1 welding procedure qualification support for welders
✔ NEBOSH IGC-aligned safety officer training for HSE personnel

What is the 4-step application process for Bongshai E&C?

1
Send Your CV — Email your updated CV to info@bongshai.com with the job title in the subject line. Include all relevant education certificates and trade qualifications as PDF attachments.
2
HR Screening — Our HR team reviews all applications within 5 business days. Shortlisted candidates are contacted by phone for a preliminary qualification review.
3
Technical Interview — Candidates invited for a face-to-face or video interview with the relevant Division Head and a Senior Engineer. Technical questions are role-specific (e.g., structural code knowledge, rigging safety, concrete testing procedures).
4
Offer & Onboarding — Selected candidates receive a written employment offer with role, salary, site allowance, and benefits details. Onboarding includes site safety induction, PPE issue, and project-specific briefing before field deployment.

What do applicants frequently ask about Bongshai E&C careers?

Do fresh graduates have opportunities at BONGSHAI E&C?
Yes. BONGSHAI E&C actively recruits B.Sc. graduates in Civil, Structural, and Electrical Engineering as Junior Site Engineers. Fresh graduates are assigned to active project sites under the direct mentoring of a Senior Engineer and receive formal code compliance and construction management training during their first 6 months.
Are positions available outside Dhaka?
Yes. BONGSHAI E&C operates project sites across Bangladesh — including Gazipur, Narayanganj, Chattogram, and other industrial hubs. Field site positions include site allowance, accommodation support, and transportation to/from site as part of the compensation package.
How long does the hiring process take?
Typically 2–3 weeks from initial CV submission to offer letter. Urgent project mobilization requirements may accelerate this timeline. Candidates with strong portfolios and relevant certifications (NEBOSH, NDT Level II, AWS D1.1 weld test) are prioritized.
