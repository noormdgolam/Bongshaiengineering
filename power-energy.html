Power & Energy Engineering Division

Bongshai Engineering & Construction provides turnkey EPC services for power and electrical infrastructure in Bangladesh. We specialize in high-voltage 33kV/11kV substations, 100 MW power plant civil execution, industrial solar PV installations, and comprehensive HT/LT electrical distribution systems.

Last updated: August 8, 2026 • Reviewed & Approved by SMA Awal, Chief Engineer, Bongshai Engineering & Construction

What power generation and electrical infrastructure services do we provide?

Bongshai Engineering & Construction provides full EPC (Engineering, Procurement, Construction) services for electrical substations, heavy industrial power cabling, grid transmission line civil foundations, and solar energy installations. Operating in compliance with Bangladesh Power Development Board (BPDB) and PGCB electrical standards, we ensure high-efficiency power distribution for industrial plants.

What are our core power engineering specializations?

33kV / 11kV Power Substation Execution

Civil transformer pad construction, oil retention pits, GIS/AIS switchgear room civil works, lightning arrester installations, and earth grid ground mat testing.

Heavy Industrial Power Plant Civil Execution

Heavy engine foundation blocks for 100 MW HFO power plants, cooling tower RCC structures, fuel tank bund walls, and heavy pipe bridge support structures.

Commercial Rooftop & Ground Solar PV

Industrial factory rooftop solar PV array framing, net-metering grid synchronization, central string inverters, and high-efficiency solar power generation.

HT / LT Cable Distribution Grids

Armored High Tension (HT) and Low Tension (LT) underground cable trench excavation, ladder-type cable tray erection, busbar trunking, and main distribution panels (MDP).

Generator Synchronization & MCC Panels

Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS) panels, multi-generator load-sharing synchronization systems, Motor Control Centers (MCC), and PLC-driven industrial automation.

High-Mast Tower & Area Lighting

20m to 30m motorized high-mast floodlight towers for sea ports, highway junctions, industrial factory laydowns, and stadium facility lighting.

What is a featured benchmark project for our Power Engineering division?

100 MW HFO Power Plant Civil Works (Katakhali, Rajshahi)

Constructed heavy engine foundation monoliths, fuel tank RCC retaining bund walls, 33kV substation yard civil structures, and heavy drainage networks for the 100 MW HFO Power Plant project in Katakhali.

How do we ensure compliance with Bangladesh power standards?

We operate in strict compliance with Bangladesh Power Development Board (BPDB) and PGCB electrical standards, ensuring that all substations, cabling, and solar PV integrations meet national safety and efficiency codes. For solar projects, we coordinate net-metering approvals with local distribution companies including BREB, DESCO, and DPDC.

What do clients frequently ask about our power engineering services?

What testing procedures are performed for 33kV substation earthing grids?

We perform 4-point Fall-of-Potential Earth Resistance Testing using calibrated Earth Testers to guarantee grounding grid resistance below 1 Ohm per IEEE 80 standards prior to energization.

Do you provide net-metering integration for industrial rooftop solar projects?

Yes, our power division handles complete net-metering approval documentation, bi-directional energy meter installation, grid protection relays, and compliance verification with local power distribution companies (BREB, DESCO, DPDC).
