Health, Safety & Environment (HSE) Policy

Bongshai Engineering & Construction enforces a zero-accident HSE policy strictly aligned with ISO 45001:2018 standards across all jobsites. We guarantee safety through mandatory PPE, daily toolbox meetings, permit-to-work systems, and strict hazard management.

Last updated: August 8, 2026 • Reviewed & Approved by SMA Awal, Chief Engineer, Bongshai Engineering & Construction

What is Bongshai E&C's corporate commitment to zero hazards?

At Bongshai Engineering & Construction, safety is not merely a checklist—it is the cornerstone of our corporate operation. In order to prevent construction accidents and guarantee that projects are executed in an environmentally sustainable manner, BONGSHAI E&C strictly enforces local Labour Law codes, BNBC guidelines, and international ISO 45001:2018 Health & Safety Standards.

It is the firm policy of executive management to do everything reasonably practicable to prevent personal injury, property damage, and environmental harm, protecting our workforce, sub-contractors, client representatives, and the general public.

What are our core HSE enforcement protocols?

What is our mandatory Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) policy?

100% hard hat, steel-toe safety boot, high-visibility vest, eye protection, and safety harness compliance required for entry onto any active project jobsite.

How do we conduct daily morning toolbox meetings?

15-minute mandatory safety briefings conducted by certified HSE officers prior to work shifts to review daily tasks, weather risks, and site hazard controls.

Fall Protection & Scaffolding Certification

Full body double-lanyard safety harnesses, heavy steel tubular scaffolding inspection tags, perimeter safety nets, and guarded floor openings above 1.8 meters height.

Permit-To-Work (PTW) System

Strict written permit authorization required prior to high-risk activities including Hot Work (welding/cutting), Deep Excavation, Confined Space Entry, and Heavy Crane Lifting.

Lockout / Tagout (LOTO) Electrical Safety

Physical locking and warning tagout procedures applied to all electrical switchgear, high voltage panels, and machinery power feeds during maintenance.

Environmental Waste & Spill Control

ISO 14001 aligned waste segregation, concrete washwater neutralization, bentonite slurry recycling, and fuel containment bunds to prevent soil/water contamination.

What do clients frequently ask about our HSE policy?

Who is responsible for safety enforcement on Bongshai project sites?

Safety is a line management responsibility. Dedicated full-time HSE Engineers reporting directly to the Project Director maintain authority to immediately stop any unsafe work activity on site.

How are near-misses and safety incidents investigated?

All near-misses and incidents undergo mandatory Root Cause Analysis (RCA) within 24 hours. Findings are documented, shared across all active sites, and integrated into corrective action plans to ensure non-recurrence.
